# Reflect core

The headless core of Reflect, in plain TypeScript with no database and no Next.js. The web app imports it as `@reflect/core/<module>`. The plan for it is `docs/design/HEADLESS_REFLECT_2026-11-10.md`: one core, with a git repository or our Postgres as the host of a person's records.

## Where things are

| Path | Holds |
|---|---|
| `src/structure.ts`, `src/structure.json` | The Lean export (`../../lean`) parsed into typed values. `structure.json` is a copy of `../../thrivesight_core/nts/structure.json`; `pnpm sync` in `web/` refreshes it. |
| `src/state.ts` | The 16 elements, their categories, salience readings and `decide`. |
| `src/person.ts`, `src/onboarding.ts` | The person's stores as pure functions over `PersonState`, and the onboarding reducer Me still uses. Mirror `../../lean/NTS/Person.lean` and `Onboarding.lean`. |
| `src/journal.ts`, `src/packs.ts`, `src/reflect.ts` | Entries and prompts, prompt packs, and reflecting with frames. |
| `src/store.ts` | The store interface, `Journal`: one person's journal, append-only, whichever host keeps it. |
| `src/layout.ts` | Where records sit in an instance folder: a vault (the lifetime folders, `journal/`, `reflections/`, `concepts/`, `documents/`, `telos/`, `intents/` at the root; `work/` ventures left to Naive) or the older `people/<id>/` layout. |
| `src/lifetime.ts` | The Ventures of a Lifetime as folders: each folder and its guide, the legacy templates, the eight needs and how each is met (`keepNeed`), the regions and their states with dated passages and the state rebuilt from them (`keepPassage`, `lifeState`), the legacy prompts, and their checks. Folders are regions and associations, never states. |
| `src/files-journal.ts` | The folder host: entries as `journal/<date>-<id>.md` (front matter optional, so a hand-written file is an entry; front matter the person adds is kept on later writes), reflections and labelings as `reflections/<entry>.json`, sparks with their concept notes in `concepts/`. |
| `src/learning.ts` | What the person knows, in `learn/`, with books at the center: books (`learn/books/`, status and dates, the subjects they cover), subjects (`learn/subjects/`, nodes of the graph with `requires`, `part_of` and `same_as`, aspire or need, and dated grasp lines the person gives: teach, explain, gist or forgot), resources (`learn/resources/`, other links, including posts and threads whose `source` (x, substack, reddit, youtube) is read from the link), films (`learn/films/`), talks (`learn/talks/`, someone the person saw speak) and conversations (`learn/conversations/`). Subjects are inputs, fields the person studies; `shown by` lines (someone saw the person display knowledge) and `suggested by` lines are never grasps. A book or film can be `referenced` (cited, how far read not said). `learnMap` derives known from the latest grasp, shown and suggested apart from it, the subjects of a read book not yet rated, and the fringe. Nothing is known until the person rates it. `learnProblems` checks it. |
| `src/ideas.ts` | The person's own ideas, outputs kept apart from learn (`ideas/`): each restatement is a dated `stated` line, inputs are `from` (the person says it fed the idea) or `maybe from` (they suspect so), and recommendations are a separate list placed `supports`, `extends`, `parallels`, `challenges` or `opposes`, with who suggested them. A target is a vault note or the person's words with an optional link. Lines are never rewritten; `foldIdea` lets the latest line about a thing decide where it stands, and the web app folds its rows with the same code. `ideaProblems` checks it. |
| `src/naive-handoff.ts` | Sending an idea to Naive on the person's say-so. Reflect never imports Naive: `sendIdea` runs the person's own `naive capture` (or `NAIVE_BIN`) in their Naive workspace (`findNaiveWorkspace` looks above the vault for `naive-workspace.json`), with `handoffText`: the title, the latest statement, the `from` inputs and a line naming the idea note. Maybe-inputs and recommendations stay in Reflect. The idea then gets a dated `sent to naive <workspace>:raw/<file>.md` line (`SENT_LINE`, read by `sentOf`). |
| `src/catalog.ts` | `index.md`, the vault's catalog, where the person's own AI starts reading. `buildIndex` lists every note by folder with one wikilinked line (ideas with their statement and inputs, subjects by grasp or want, books, films, talks, conversations, links, concepts, ventures under `work/`, sources) and the folder guides; journal entries are listed by date only, never their text. `writeIndex` rewrites it and reports whether anything changed. It is derived from the notes and holds no fact of its own. |
| `src/wiki.ts` | The wiki: a compiled view of the vault written by the person's own AI, kept apart from the record (after Farza's personal wiki skill). `wikiOn` turns it on (off by default). `wikiPrompt` gives the AI the writer's instructions, `index.md`, the wiki so far and up to 15 journal entries not yet absorbed (pointer entries read live). `readWikiReply` checks each article (`checkArticle`): a `<folder>/<slug>` path, no em dash, every paragraph, list item and table row citing a note in the record (a "See also" line of wiki links is exempt), and every quote of four words or more found in what its passage cites; refused articles are listed with why, and the rest wait as a draft in `.reflect/`. `keepWikiDraft` writes the chosen articles (front matter with type, aliases, related, cited sources), logs absorbed entries in `wiki/_absorb.json` and rebuilds `wiki/_index.md` and `wiki/_backlinks.json`. A pattern, tension, identity, philosophy, life theme, assessment or metaphor is `reading: ai` until `confirmReading`; the confirmation holds for that text's hash, so a rewrite makes it a reading again. A pattern, tension or identity also names its elements and must answer to the element table (`groundProblem` in `ground.ts`); the prompt lists each entry's confirmed elements so the AI can. With a soul vault, those three are kept in its `of-me/`, not the wiki. `wikiProblems` names an article citing a note that left the vault, or a reading that no longer answers to the table. |
| `src/soul.ts` | The soul vault: the inner life in a vault of its own, in the terms of the Number Theory of the Soul. `initSoul` starts one beside a life vault (never inside it), with `soul.json` (Image: the person), `table/structure.json` (the element table, pinned), `moments/`, `readings/`, `bindings/`, `holds/` and `of-me/`, and moves the journal into it: entries become moments and reflections become readings, adopted into the soul vault's memory. The two `reflect.json` files name each other (`soul`, `life`). `soulStatus` counts what it holds; `soulProblems` checks the pair links back, then bindings, holds and of-me through `ground.ts`. |
| `src/ground.ts` | The soul vault answering to the element table. `bindMoment` and `unbindMoment` keep `bindings/<moment>.json`: what a moment was about, as a path in the life vault (a note or folder that exists, with an optional thematic role), append-only, with an unbind kept as a line. `noteProfile` counts, for a life note or folder, the elements the person confirmed (or added) on the moments bound to it, by each moment's latest labeling; it is computed when asked and never stored, and model guesses never count. `keepHold` and `listHolds` keep `holds/<name>.md`: a belief or value in the person's words, with how they hold it (desire, will, duty or sacred, each facing an element), a new stated line each time. `groundProblem` is the rule for readings of the person: a pattern, tension or identity names its elements, a tension is the two elements of one category, and every moment it cites has a confirmed reading showing every element it names. `ofMeProblems`, `boundProblems` and `holdProblems` run in `reflect check`. `boundNotes`, `ofMeReadings` and `elementTotals` feed the screens of `reflect serve`. |
| `src/home.ts` | `journalHome`: where a vault's journal lives, its own folder or the soul vault it links to, with the life vault for sources and concept notes. The CLI, the catalog, the wiki, sources and the release plan read and write entries through it, so the journal works from either folder. |
| `src/sources.ts` | The person's other folders, pointed at instead of moved in. `addSource` names a folder outside the vault in `sources.json`; `indexSource` fingerprints its files into `.reflect/index/` (derived, git-ignored), leaving out hidden folders, dependencies, exclusions and names that look like secrets. `placementPrompt` asks the person's own AI where each open file belongs (book, film, talk, link, conversation, subject, idea, entry, venture, document or none); `readPlacements` reads its reply, keeping only files in the index; `keepPlacements` turns each chosen one into a dated `pointer <source>:<path> #<sha>` line on its note and a line in `sources/<name>.md`. An entry (a plain text file) becomes a closed journal entry whose front matter is `pointer: <source>:<path>` and `pointer_sha`; `filesJournal` reads its text from the file each time (`pointerText`), so it is listed, reflected on and labeled without a copy, and placing it again keeps the same entry. A venture maps to its Naive folder `work/<slug>` when there is one (Reflect never writes inside it); without one, a venture with a statement is kept as an idea with the pointer, and one with neither is only mapped, with a note saying so. A document becomes a custody card whose `where` is the file. Nothing in a source is moved, edited or copied. `sourceStatus` and `sourceProblems` report placed files that changed or vanished. |
| `src/sweep.ts` | The memory sweep: `SWEEP_PROMPT` for the person's own AI, `readSweep` for its JSON reply (`reflect.learn-source` v1), and `keepSweep` for the items the person chose. Inputs and ideas are kept apart; an idea's input the AI only inferred is kept as maybe from; nothing sets a grasp. |
| `src/openlibrary.ts` | Looking a book up in Open Library: `searchBooks` by title or author, `lookupIsbn` for one edition with its work's subjects and table of contents, both over an injected fetch. The catalog is editable by anyone, so its entry is a suggestion saved beside the book note (`learn/books/<book>.openlibrary.json`); the book note changes only by the person. |
| `src/transcript.ts` | A transcript as courses: `draftTranscript` asks a model for rows and `anchorCourses` keeps only rows whose title (and grade) are in the text; `keepCourse` writes a confirmed row to `learn/courses/` linked to a subject. The grade is the school's; grasp is the person's. |
| `src/credentials.ts` | Credentials (`learn/credentials/`), with `checkBadge` reading an Open Badges assertion; vouches as dated lines on a subject (`keepVouches`); recommendations kept whole (`learn/vouches/`). None set grasp. |
| `src/linkedin.ts` | Reads a LinkedIn data export: endorsements, recommendations, certifications and listed skills, columns found by name (`parseCsv`, `linkedInDate`, `readLinkedInExport`). |
| `src/documents.ts` | Official documents as a custody checklist: the catalog of document types (grouped after FEMA's Emergency Financial First Aid Kit), how each is normally kept as of 2026-11 in the United States and what we recommend, custody cards (`documents/<type>.md`), connections (`connections/<id>.json`, a service and the types it holds, never credentials) and their checks. |
| `src/record-io.ts` | Writes one record and logs it in `memory.jsonl`. |
| `src/release.ts` | The release plan (`legacy/release.json`): trustees, confirmations, the wait, and gifts, with the hard rule that only `legacy/` and entries marked `legacy: true` can be given (`giftRefusal`), and `previewRelease`, what any release would hand each person. The Reflect app carries a release out (`../../web/src/server/legacy-store.ts`); a person's AI sends the plan with `keep_legacy_plan`. |
| `src/spark.ts` | Object writing: the forms (1, 5, 10, 15 minutes or one sentence), concrete nouns to start from, the prompt, and a spark's front matter and concept note. |
| `src/memory.ts` | Memory: `memory.jsonl`, one hash-chained line per write (`verifyMemory` finds a changed or removed line). |
| `src/journal-contract.ts` | The behaviour every host must have. The folder host runs it in `files-journal.test.ts`, and the Postgres host (`postgresJournal` in `../../web/src/server/journal-store.ts`) in its own tests against Neon. |
| `src/instance.ts` | An instance in a folder: `initInstance` (writes the vault and `template/reflect.yml` as the instance's workflow), `readConfig`, `checkInstance`, `adopt`. |
| `src/reader.ts` | The reader: Jev's element odds and the basis of each self-term, gpt-oss to find self-terms. The web app adds the Vercel OIDC token. |
| `src/draft.ts` | A draft reflection (`makeDraft`) and what keeping it records (`keepDraft`). |
| `src/bundle.ts` | A bundle: one person's journal as one JSON document, the same from either host. Moving hosts is an export from one and an import into the other's empty journal. |
| `src/bindings.ts` | Concepts and bindings. A binding ties one element, at a weight, to one or more concepts the entry names: an entity (a noun: a person, place, thing, group or idea), an event (a verb: something that happened or was done), a quality (an adjective or adverb: how something was) or a time. Each attachment keeps the entry's own words for the concept ("left" for the concept "leave") and, when given, its role (agent, patient, place, time, manner, cause, goal). A reading is the bindings kept together at one time; the first is as felt then and later ones never replace it. `bindingProblems` checks a reading, `conceptKey` is a concept's identity (kind, sort, name), `asFeltThen` and `asOf` pick a reading, and `presentMeaning` fades a concept's history by element (the half-lives are parameters, not findings). The Postgres store is `../../web/src/server/binding-store.ts`; the folder host does not keep bindings yet (not built). |
| `src/theme.ts` | The app's themes. |
| `src/salience-prompt.ts` | Generated from `../../thrivesight_core/nts/prompts/salience.md`. Do not edit. |

Modules import each other by their `.ts` names and import JSON with `with { type: "json" }`, so Node runs them directly (`node -e 'import("./src/state.ts")'`, Node 22.18 or later).

## Tests

`pnpm test` in `web/` runs `src/*.test.ts` with vitest. The app still calls the Postgres store's functions directly.
